Who We Are

An evangelical ministry called to the nations.

Global Endtime Harvest is an evangelical ministry called to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ across nations and participate in the end-time harvest of souls.

Under the leadership of Evangelist Precious Olawole Ologunloluwa, GEH is committed to reaching villages, towns, cities and nations with the Gospel.

Our ministry combines Gospel proclamation with practical expressions of Christian love through Gospel crusades, evangelism, church mobilisation, discipleship and follow-up, prayer and healing ministry, medical outreaches, feeding programmes, welfare support and missionary work.

Our desire is not simply to gather people, but to see lives reached with the Gospel of Jesus Christ and believers strengthened to participate in the Great Commission.

MISSION

“To go into all the world and preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to every creature.”

Mark 16:15 KJV

Global Endtime Harvest exists to proclaim Jesus Christ across nations, call people to salvation and faith in Him, pray for the sick, make disciples, mobilise the Church and demonstrate the love of Christ through practical acts of service.

VISION

“To proclaim God’s Kingdom across all nations and bring in the end-time harvest of souls.”

We envision communities, cities and nations reached with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, believers established in the faith, churches working together for the advancement of God’s Kingdom, and people prepared for the return of Jesus Christ.

Lead Evangelist & Founder

A voice for the Gospel among the nations.

Evangelist Precious Ologunloluwa is a Nigerian Christian minister, author and global evangelist. He serves as the Lead Evangelist of Global Endtime Harvest, a ministry dedicated to Gospel proclamation, soul-winning, revival and preparing people for the coming of Jesus Christ.

He is passionate about reaching people with the Gospel and has been involved in organising Gospel crusades and evangelical outreaches across Nigeria.

Through Global Endtime Harvest, he combines Gospel preaching with practical expressions of Christian love, including prayer for the sick, free medical outreaches and welfare support for widows and the less privileged.

He also shares Gospel messages, video devotions and biblical content through digital platforms, reaching people locally and internationally.

Our Crusades

Gospel-centred gatherings that go beyond the event.

Our Gospel crusades give communities the opportunity to hear the message of Jesus Christ, respond to the Gospel and encounter the love of God.

Through collaboration with local churches, Christian leaders, volunteers and ministry partners, we seek to create Gospel-centred gatherings that extend beyond the event itself through prayer, follow-up and discipleship.

Overview

Three days of Gospel and love in Ilaro.

The Ilaro Jesus Crusade was a three-day Gospel outreach organised by Global Endtime Harvest in collaboration with the Christian Association of Nigeria (CAN), Yewa South Local Government.

The outreach focused on proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serving the community through practical expressions of Christian love. Food support was also provided to hundreds of widows.

Elementor (Free) Build Map

Layout
  • Enable Flexbox Containers. Boxed width 1200px, side padding 24px (mobile 16px).
  • Section padding: 110 / 84 / 64px (desktop / tablet / mobile).
  • Photo heroes: Container → Background Image + Background Overlay (#08080A, gradient 92%→35% or flat 74%).
  • Entrance animation: Motion Effects → Fade In Up, duration Normal, delay 0/100/200/300ms for staggered cards.
  • Hover lift on cards: Advanced → Transform (hover) translateY −6px + Box Shadow.
Widgets used (all free)
  • Heading, Text Editor, Button, Image, Icon Box, Icon List, Divider, Spacer.
  • Video (YouTube), Basic Gallery (lightbox), Accordion (FAQ), Tabs (media filter), Social Icons.
  • Copy buttons on Give page: HTML widget with the inline script at the bottom of this file (no external code).
Header & footer (free route)
  • Theme: Hello Elementor. Plugin: Ultimate Addons “Elementor Header & Footer Builder” (free) for header/footer templates + its free Navigation Menu widget (dropdowns + mobile hamburger).
  • Sticky header: Header container → Advanced → Position/Z-index, or the plugin’s sticky option.
  • Mobile bottom action bar: Container, Position Fixed bottom 0, visible on mobile only (Responsive → Hide on Desktop/Tablet).
Forms & content rules
  • Elementor Form is Pro, so use MetForm or WPForms Lite (free) for the Invitation & Contact forms. Match the field list shown.
  • No attendance, decision or healing numbers anywhere (per brief).
  • No invented dates or cities for the next crusade.
  • Compress photos to WebP ≤ 250 KB (hero 1920px wide, cards 1200px) before upload.
  • Phone in international format: +234 903 744 4800 / wa.me/2349037444800.
Paystack partnership setup

  • Create a Plan (interval: Monthly) for each tier: ₦2,000, ₦5,000, ₦10,000, ₦20,000, ₦50,000, ₦100,000 (all linked), plus a Subscription payment page for each. Paste each page URL into its tier button (Button widget → Link).
