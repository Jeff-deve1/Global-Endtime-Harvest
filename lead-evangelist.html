Meet Our Lead Evangelist

Evangelist Precious Olawole Ologunloluwa

Home/About/Lead Evangelist
Lead Evangelist & Founder

A voice for the Gospel among the nations.

Evangelist Precious Ologunloluwa is a Nigerian Christian minister, author and global evangelist. He serves as the Lead Evangelist of Global Endtime Harvest, a ministry dedicated to Gospel proclamation, soul-winning, revival and preparing people for the coming of Jesus Christ.

He is passionate about reaching people with the Gospel and has been involved in organising Gospel crusades and evangelical outreaches across Nigeria.

Through Global Endtime Harvest, he combines Gospel preaching with practical expressions of Christian love, including prayer for the sick, free medical outreaches and welfare support for widows and the less privileged.

He also shares Gospel messages, video devotions and biblical content through digital platforms, reaching people locally and internationally.
