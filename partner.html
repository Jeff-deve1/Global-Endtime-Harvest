Your Impact · How Your Gifts Fuel the Mission

The Great Commission belongs to the whole body of Christ.

Not everyone may travel to every city or stand behind a pulpit, but everyone can take part in reaching the nations.

Through prayer, giving, volunteering and strategic partnership, you can become part of the mission of Global Endtime Harvest.

What partnership supports

  • Gospel crusades
  • Evangelistic outreaches
  • Prayer & healing ministry
  • Medical outreaches
  • Feeding programmes
  • Widows & less privileged
  • Missionary activities
  • Gospel resources
  • Discipleship & follow-up
  • Ministry logistics
