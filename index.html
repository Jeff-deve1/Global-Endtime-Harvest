Taking the Gospel to the Nations.

Proclaiming Christ. Reaching the Nations. Preparing the Harvest.

Global Endtime Harvest exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ across villages, towns, cities and nations, calling people to salvation, praying for the sick, making disciples and demonstrating the love of Christ through practical outreach.

The Mandate

The Gospel must reach the nations.

Global Endtime Harvest has been called to take the Gospel of Jesus Christ to villages, towns, cities and nations: proclaiming salvation through Christ, praying for the sick, making disciples and demonstrating the love of Christ.

Our mandate is rooted in the Great Commission of Mark 16 and the end-time promise of Matthew 24:14.

Read Our Mandate
“

Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.

Mark 16:15 KJV

And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.

Matthew 24:14 KJV
Our Mission

To go into all the world and preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to every creature.

We proclaim Jesus Christ across nations, call people to salvation, pray for the sick, make disciples, mobilise the Church and demonstrate the love of Christ through practical service.

Our Vision

To proclaim God’s Kingdom across all nations and bring in the end-time harvest of souls.

What We Do

One Gospel. Many expressions of one mission.

Every expression of our ministry flows from, and points back to, the proclamation of Jesus Christ.

Gospel Crusades

Gospel-centred crusades and evangelistic gatherings that take the message of Jesus Christ to communities, cities and nations.

Evangelism

Public and personal evangelism, reaching people everywhere with the message of salvation.

Prayer & Healing

We pray for the sick and trust God for healing as we proclaim Jesus Christ.

Discipleship

Helping those who respond to the Gospel to grow in faith and become established in Christ.

Humanitarian Outreach

Free medical outreaches, food support and welfare for widows and communities in need.

Church Mobilisation

Working with churches and leaders to mobilise believers around the Great Commission.

Meet Our Lead Evangelist

Evangelist Precious Olawole Ologunloluwa

Nigerian Christian minister, author and global evangelist, and Lead Evangelist of Global Endtime Harvest.

He is passionate about reaching people with the Gospel and has organised Gospel crusades and evangelical outreaches across Nigeria, combining Gospel preaching with prayer for the sick, free medical outreaches and welfare support for widows and the less privileged.
